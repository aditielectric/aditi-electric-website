About Aditi Electric

Aditi Electric is a trusted electrical and electronics store in Surat, offering a wide range of products for homes, shops, offices and commercial requirements. We deal in air coolers, LED TVs, home theatre systems, RO purifiers, mixer grinders, switches, sockets, LED lights, bulbs, electrical wires, cables, fittings and other electrical accessories. Located at Sarthee Heights, Punagam, Surat, we aim to provide genuine products, competitive prices and helpful service for your electrical and electronics needs. Visit Aditi Electric for reliable products and helpful support in Punagam and surrounding areas of Surat.
